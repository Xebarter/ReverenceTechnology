Reverence Technology

Level 2 — Geography

Uganda → East Africa → Africa

↓

For example:

School Management Software → Rwanda

Mobile App Development → Kenya

ERP Development → Tanzania

Business Automation → Uganda

E-commerce Development → East Africa

Custom Software → Africa

That gives you a scalable SEO architecture instead of a random keyword collection.

The regional positioning I would use

I would gradually move the company's positioning from:

Reverence Technology — Software Development Company in Uganda

to something along the lines of:

Reverence Technology — African Software Development & Digital Solutions Company

while still maintaining strong Uganda/Kampala landing pages.

That lets Google understand that Uganda is your home market, East Africa is your regional market, and Africa is your broader market.

Most importantly, don't create hundreds of thin pages just by swapping country names. Each regional page should contain genuinely useful, localized content, relevant examples, industries, payment systems, regulations, business needs and case studies for that market. Otherwise, the pages can look like doorway pages rather than valuable regional resources.
